Both course records fell at the city marathon on Sunday as runners took advantage of cool, still conditions that coaches described as close to perfect.

Daniel Kiprop won the men's race in 2:03:41, more than a minute inside the previous best, after breaking clear of a leading group of six at 35 kilometres. In the women's race, Selam Haile ran a remarkably even pace to finish in 2:17:55.

"At the start it was nine degrees and there was no wind at all," Haile said. "You wait your whole career for a day like this."

More than 40,000 runners finished the race, a record for the event, and organisers said more than £9m had been raised for charity.
