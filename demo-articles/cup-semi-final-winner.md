Harbour Town are going to the cup final. Three years after they were playing in front of a few hundred fans in the fourth tier, the club completed one of the most improbable runs in the competition's history with a 2-1 win over Northfield Rovers, secured by a header in the 94th minute.

The winner came from a corner that had no business being awarded, according to Northfield's furious manager. Jess Okoro rose highest at the back post and glanced the ball into the far corner, sending the travelling fans behind the goal into delirium.

"I didn't even see it go in," Okoro said afterwards. "I just heard the noise."

## A game of two halves

Northfield had dominated the first half and led through a fine strike from distance, but Harbour Town came out transformed after the break. Their equaliser, a flowing move finished by the substitute Leon Marsh, was the least they deserved.

The final will be played next month. Harbour Town's opponents will be decided in the second semi-final on Sunday.
