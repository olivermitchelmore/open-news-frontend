Organisers of cycling's biggest stage race have unveiled a route for next season that includes six summit finishes, two individual time trials and what may be the hardest single day of racing in the event's history.

The penultimate stage will cross four major passes and finish at the top of a climb that has never before featured in the race, accumulating more than 5,000 metres of climbing over 180 kilometres.

"It is beautiful on paper," said one team director, who asked not to be named. "On a bike, in the third week, it is going to be brutal."

## Built for climbers

The route favours pure climbers over all-rounders, with only 45 kilometres of time trialling in total. Several of the favourites are expected to adjust their season to arrive at the race in peak climbing form.

Race organisers said they had deliberately designed a route that would keep the overall result uncertain until the final weekend.
